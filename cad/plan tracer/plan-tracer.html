Help
  1. Floor: set the floor ID, level, frame and units per meter.
  2. Underlays: pick one or more images. Choose one in the list, then drag it with the Underlay tool or type x, y, rotation and scale. Flip, opacity, Visible and Locked apply to the chosen one. Crop: drag a rectangle on it with the Crop tool.
  3. Calibrate: enter the real distance in meters, then click two points a known distance apart on the underlay. The underlay is rescaled to match.
  4. Line: click the start, then the end, or drag from the start to the end. Ends snap to line ends, midpoints, intersections and the grid. After the start you can type a length in meters and press Enter. Ortho (the button on the drawing, the checkbox, or Shift) locks 45° steps; it makes rectangles square and keeps moves and line-end drags on one axis. Chain lines continues from the last end. Esc cancels.
  5. Rectangle: click two opposite corners. Measure: click two points to read the distance. Dimension: click two points, then where the dimension line goes.
  6. Offset: select lines, set the distance in meters, choose Offset and click the side. Trim: click the part of a line to remove between its crossings. Extend: click a line near the end to lengthen it to the next line.
  7. Line thickness and color apply to new lines; use Apply to selected lines to change existing ones. Edit has Select all (Ctrl+A) and Join collinear; Arrange aligns and distributes the selection.
  8. Text: type it in the Text field, choose the Text tool, click where it goes.
  9. Select: click an element, Shift-click to add, drag on empty space to select a box, drag to move, drag a line end to reshape. Select one text to edit it and use Apply to selected text. Delete, Duplicate, Undo and Redo work on the selection.
  10. Pan: Pan tool, middle mouse button, or two fingers. Zoom: wheel, pinch or the Zoom buttons.
  11. Right-click ends a chain or cancels a pending point. The marker at the cursor shows the snap: square = end, triangle = midpoint, cross = intersection, plus = grid.
  12. Export PNG saves the frame area as a picture. Theme cycles system, light and dark. Click a section title to collapse it.
  13. Work is autosaved in this browser. Save writes the plan SVG; untick "Include underlay reference" to leave the images out. Open reads a plan SVG; pick the underlay images again to see them.
