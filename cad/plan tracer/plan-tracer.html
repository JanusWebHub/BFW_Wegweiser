Help
  1. Floor: set the floor ID, level, frame and units per meter.
  2. Underlays: pick one or more images. Choose one in the list, then drag it with the Underlay tool or type x, y, rotation and scale. Flip, opacity, Visible and Locked apply to the chosen one. Crop: drag a rectangle on it with the Crop tool.
  3. Underlay tools: Rotate turns the chosen underlay about the center of its visible part as you drag (Shift: 15° steps). Make horizontal: click two points that should be level and the underlay turns about the first. The arrow buttons nudge it by the step. Raise and Lower change which underlay is drawn on top, Name sets a label, Brightness and Contrast adjust the picture. All are saved with the underlay.
  4. View: Zoom window zooms to a dragged rectangle. Map shows an overview, and clicking or dragging in it moves the view. The rulers read meters from the frame's top-left corner, and the scale bar shows a round length.
  5. Calibrate: enter the real distance in meters, then click two points a known distance apart on the underlay. The underlay is rescaled to match.
  6. Line: click the start, then the end, or drag from the start to the end. Ends snap to line ends, midpoints, intersections and the grid. After the start you can type a length in meters and press Enter. Ortho (the button on the drawing, the checkbox, or Shift) locks 45° steps; it makes rectangles square and keeps moves and line-end drags on one axis. Chain lines continues from the last end. Esc cancels.
  7. Rectangle: click two opposite corners. Measure: click two points to read the distance. Dimension: click two points, then where the dimension line goes.
  8. Polyline and Path: click the points, then right-click, Enter or a second click on the last point to finish (Path can be a smooth curve, open or closed). Circle: click the center, then a point on the circle. Select a shape to drag its vertices or its radius. Every element has an ID, shown and renamable in Edit.
  9. Symbols: choose a type, set the width and depth in meters, then click with Place symbol; the symbol is centered on the click. Select one to change its size, type or rotation, or turn it by 90°.
  10. Text on an element: with the Text tool, a click on a line, polyline, path, circle or symbol attaches the text to it (the option can be switched off); it then follows the element, lies along lines and can be dragged to set its offset. Select one text and one element and use Attach text or Detach text to change it later. Deleting an element deletes its attached texts.
  11. Layers: add, rename and delete layers, hide or lock the chosen one, and move the selected elements to it. New elements go to the chosen layer. Hidden layers are not drawn, snapped to or exported; locked layers cannot be picked.
  12. Offset: select lines, set the distance in meters, choose Offset and click the side. Trim: click the part of a line to remove between its crossings. Extend: click a line near the end to lengthen it to the next line.
  13. Line thickness and color apply to new lines; use Apply to selected lines to change existing ones. Edit has Select all (Ctrl+A) and Join collinear; Arrange aligns and distributes the selection.
  14. Text: type it in the Text field, choose the Text tool, click where it goes.
  15. Select: click an element, Shift-click to add, drag on empty space to select a box, drag to move, drag a line end to reshape. Select one text to edit it and use Apply to selected text. Delete, Duplicate, Undo and Redo work on the selection.
  16. Pan: Pan tool, middle mouse button, or two fingers. Zoom: wheel, pinch or the Zoom buttons.
  17. Right-click ends a chain or cancels a pending point. The marker at the cursor shows the snap: square = end, triangle = midpoint, X = intersection, plus = grid, circle = aligned with another point, bar with ticks = extension of a line, right angle = perpendicular, two slashes = parallel, diamond = polar angle.
  18. Tracking guides (dashed green, switched by the checkbox): the cursor snaps level with the ends and midpoints of lines and with the start point, onto the extension of a line beyond its end, and where two guides cross. While drawing it also snaps to the perpendicular foot on a line, parallel to a line you hovered over, and to 0, 30, 45, 60, 90 degrees and so on around the start point. Ortho turns the tracking off while a line is being drawn.
  19. Export PNG saves the frame area as a picture. Theme cycles system, light and dark. Click a section title to collapse it.
  20. Work is autosaved in this browser. Save writes the plan SVG; untick "Include underlay reference" to leave the images out. Open reads a plan SVG; pick the underlay images again to see them.
