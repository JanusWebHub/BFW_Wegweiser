Help
  1. Floor: set the floor ID, level, frame and units per meter.
  2. Underlays: pick one or more images. Choose one in the list, then drag it with the Underlay tool or type x, y, rotation and scale. Flip, opacity, Visible and Locked apply to the chosen one. Crop: drag a rectangle on it with the Crop tool.
  3. Calibrate: enter the real distance in meters, then click two points a known distance apart on the underlay. The underlay is rescaled to match.
  4. Line: click the start, then the end. Ends snap to line ends, midpoints, intersections and the grid. Ortho (or Shift) locks 45° steps. Chain lines continues from the last end. Esc cancels.
  5. Rectangle: click two opposite corners. Measure: click two points to read the distance.
  6. Text: type it in the Text field, choose the Text tool, click where it goes.
  7. Select: click an element, Shift-click to add, drag on empty space to select a box, drag to move, drag a line end to reshape. Select one text to edit it and use Apply to selected text. Delete, Duplicate, Undo and Redo work on the selection.
  8. Pan: Pan tool, middle mouse button, or two fingers. Zoom: wheel, pinch or the Zoom buttons.
  9. Work is autosaved in this browser. Save writes the plan SVG; untick "Include underlay reference" to leave the images out. Open reads a plan SVG; pick the underlay images again to see them.
